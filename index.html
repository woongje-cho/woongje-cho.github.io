Lab notes

At MLCS Lab, Yonsei (advisor Prof. Jongeun Choi), I worked on giving LLM agents structured world knowledge so their plans hold up in big, messy places.

NeurIPS 2026 · Main Track

OntoPlan: An Ontology-Grounded Scene Representation and Agentic Framework for Scalable Robot Task Planning

A multi-agent LLM framework that grounds planning in an ontology knowledge graph and checks plans with PDDL. 0.79 goal success against 0.30 for SayPlan, with about 12.7x fewer tokens.

Co-author. My part: agent prompt strategy, few-shot evaluation, multi-turn and ambiguous-instruction handling, and the 3D scene-graph evaluation setup.

KSAE 2026 · First author

Knowledge Graph-Based Semantic Scene Understanding for Autonomous Driving in Dilemma Situations

4.41 vs 3.56

My graduation research. In driving dilemmas, an ontology knowledge graph gave an LLM better judgment than a 3D scene graph on a five-point scale (N=300, p<0.001).
